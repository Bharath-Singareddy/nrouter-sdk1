# Work type 04 — company position

**Category:** `company/` · **Frontmatter `category`:** `Company`
**Length:** 1,800–2,400 words · **Links:** 12+ (7+ internal)

## Required sections, in order

1. `## The position` — blockquote, one sentence, unhedged.
2. `## What we were asked for instead` — the request we keep declining, stated fairly and
   at its strongest. A position argued against a weak version of the ask persuades nobody.
3. `## What that would cost you` — the concrete downside, with a number or a named failure.
4. `## What we do instead` — the mechanism, linked to the product surface that implements it.
5. `## The trade we are making` — what our choice genuinely costs the customer. Required.
6. `## Who this is wrong for` — the segment that should pick a different vendor. Name them.
7. `## How you can hold us to it` — the observable proof: the ledger, the header, the
   pricing page, the status page, the contract.
8. `## Try it` → `## See also` → `## Sources`.

## Proof obligation

- A position post asserts nothing about competitors that is not linked and dated.
- Every promise ties to something a customer can check without asking us.
- Link the other company posts; the stance cluster is the argument, and each post alone is
  a fragment of it.
