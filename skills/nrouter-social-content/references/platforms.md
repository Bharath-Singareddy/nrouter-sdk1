# Platform content adaptation

Platform formats change. Verify current limits in the platform's official documentation when exact limits affect the requested copy.

## LinkedIn

- Use a professional opening, short paragraphs, and a clear technical takeaway.
- Keep hashtags relevant and limited; do not turn the post into a tag list.
- Make the result ready for a person to review, copy, and publish manually.

## Instagram

- Write a concise caption with readable spacing, a clear call to action, useful alt text, and restrained hashtags.
- Generate caption text only. Image and video creation are outside this skill.

## X

- Prefer one focused post. Use a numbered thread only when the content cannot fit responsibly in one post.
- Check the current character, media, and link behavior before publishing.
- Make the result ready for manual posting.

## YouTube

- Produce a concise Community post and reusable description copy with a clear opening and CTA.
- Keep titles, descriptions, links and hashtags consistent with the approved campaign message.
- Written content only: do not generate video scripts, scenes, shots, visual prompts or video assets.
- Make the result ready for manual posting.

## Other destinations

For another named platform, establish its current content format and adapt the draft accordingly. Do not call its publishing API.

## Manual fallback

When a connector is unavailable, return:

- final platform-specific copy;
- verified links;
- a concise manual posting checklist.
