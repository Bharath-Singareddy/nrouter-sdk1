# nRouter Brand Identity & Logos

## Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** Deep Navy (#0F172A)
- **Accents & Gradients:** Vibrant Electric Blue and Violet/Purple.
- **Highlights:** Soft Cyan neon glow effects.

## Visual Aesthetic & Logos
- **Logo:** The nRouter logo is a sleek, angular geometric gateway, often depicted with glowing data streams. 
- **Style:** Clean, modern, technical flat vector art or stylized abstract 3D.
- **Restrictions:** NO Photorealism, NO generic corporate stock photos, NO humans.
